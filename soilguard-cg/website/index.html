AGRONOMIC EMERGENCY

The Chhattisgarh Topsoil Degradation Crisis

Quantified breakdown of soil nutrient depletion in the Chhattisgarh plains (Vertisols & Inceptisols) and the limitations of physical sampling.

📉

~52% Carbon Deficient

Over half of agricultural fields in the Chhattisgarh plain suffer from low Soil Organic Carbon (< 0.5% SOC), causing severe structural aggregate breakdown and topsoil baking during dry post-harvest periods.

⚠️

~77% Nitrogen Depletion

Soil Health Card assays reveal near-zero available nitrogen in ~77% of sample grids across Raipur and Durg districts, forcing chemical over-application and groundwater vulnerability.

🔬

Spatial Sampling Gap

Traditional Soil Health Cards sample 1 point per 10 hectares once every 3 years. This coarse physical sampling completely misses intra-field spatial variability during dry seasons.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

6-Stage Geospatial Processing Pipeline

Automated end-to-end telemetry pipeline converting satellite Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs into actionable block-level agronomic advisories.

01 / STAC
Sentinel-2 L2A
Windowed read of B02, B04, B08, B11 from Earth-Search COG.
02 / SPECTRAL
BSI + NDVI
Calculates BSI and NDVI; applies NDVI ≤ 0.30 bare soil mask.
03 / ML MODEL
Random Forest
100% Satellite spectral feature regressor (150 trees).
04 / RASTER
10m Risk Map
Reconstructs 2D risk score & ensemble uncertainty maps.
05 / ZONAL
5×5 Grid Ranking
Aggregates risk into 25 spatial sectors & ranks priorities.
06 / ADVISORY
Agronomic Plan
Generates sector-specific FYM, Biochar & Lime advisory packages.
TARGET LEAKAGE PREVENTION GUARANTEE

To ensure genuine satellite spectral learning, raw SoilGrids SOC was 100% EXCLUDED from the feature matrix X. The Random Forest model predicts degradation risk strictly from Sentinel-2 spectral channels (SWIR-1, NIR, Red, Blue, and ratios).

y_proxy = 0.50 × (1 - SOC_norm) + 0.50 × BSI_norm
